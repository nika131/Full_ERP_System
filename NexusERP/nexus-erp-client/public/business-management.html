What can you manage with TenexERP?

Inventory management

Organize products, review stock quantities, record inventory changes and keep track of low-stock items.

Point of sale

Record in-store sales, calculate totals with backend validation, manage discounts according to role permissions and view receipts.

Multiple stores

Maintain stores in one company workspace and review the operations associated with each location.

Sales insights

Review sales activity and business performance reports to stay informed about day-to-day operations.

Team permissions

Manage employees and roles, define what each person can access, and use POS PIN access for in-store staff.

Cashier shifts

Open and close shifts, record cash movements when permitted, and review shift records.

Manage from anywhere: Stay connected to your sales, inventory, and business operations through the online dashboard, without needing to be physically present at your store all day.

Who is TenexERP for?

TenexERP helps shops, retailers, and teams managing products, sales and multiple locations bring their everyday operations together. Whether you are organizing your first store or coordinating several, TenexERP provides a connected set of tools without the spreadsheet juggling.

How do you get started?

  1. Create a TenexERP account and company workspace.
  2. Add stores and products to organize inventory.
  3. Assign employee permissions and set up the POS app for in-store transactions.

Frequently asked questions

Can TenexERP manage inventory and point of sale together?

Yes. Product records, store operations, sales and receipts work together within the TenexERP platform.

Can different employees have different permissions?

Yes. Administrators can assign roles with permissions for employee operations, sales, discounts, store management and more.

Is TenexERP designed for small businesses?

Yes. Its tools are designed for small teams and businesses managing stock, stores and everyday sales, while supporting growing operations.

Is an Android POS application available?

TenexERP includes a React Native POS application for Android in-store operations alongside its web dashboard. Check the website for current availability.
